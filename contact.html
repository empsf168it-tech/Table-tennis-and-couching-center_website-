Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We've got answers.

What are the academy's operating hours?

Our training centers are open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM, and Saturdays & Sundays from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM.

How can I reach the coaching staff directly?

You can submit an inquiry form specifying your level of play, call our support number at +1 (555) 774-6888, or visit any of our training venues during training hours.

Do you host corporate events or group bookings?

Yes, we offer custom packages for corporate team building, private leagues, and birthday parties, which include paddle rentals, tournament coordination, and exhibition matches.

Is there parking available at the training centers?

Yes, all our training centers have free on-site parking for members and visitors.
